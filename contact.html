HOW TO WRITE

Good routing starts with good context.

A clear subject line and the relevant desk make it easier to understand the purpose of your communication.

01

Choose the desk

Use the closest category instead of sending every enquiry to the general office inbox.

02

Add the essentials

For published material, include the relevant URL, title or reference wherever possible.

03

Protect sensitive data

Do not send confidential or unnecessary personal information with an initial enquiry.

04

Verify official details

For court orders, hearings and case status, independently verify the latest position with the concerned official source.
