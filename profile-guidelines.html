Profile Guidelines

These guidelines are designed to keep Lex Talk Legal professional profiles informational, accurate, moderated and security-conscious.

What the profile is

A free informational professional profile containing factual professional information submitted by the profile holder. Publication is subject to review.

What should not be submitted

No “best lawyer”, “top lawyer” or paid-ranking claims.
No guaranteed results, success percentages or misleading case-outcome claims.
No testimonials, ratings or comparative claims in the profile.
No threats, abusive content, impersonation or false credentials.
No confidential client information or private documents.
No content that violates law, professional-conduct rules or third-party rights.

Review process

01Submit
02Administrative Review
03Clarification if Needed
04Publish / Decline

Search engines

Approved profiles may be made discoverable to search engines. Search-engine crawling, indexing and rankings are controlled by the relevant search engine; Lex Talk Legal does not guarantee indexing, ranking or traffic.

Important professional-conduct note

Lex Talk Legal is designed to provide informational professional profiles rather than a paid legal-service marketplace. Profile holders remain responsible for complying with the professional-conduct rules applicable to them. Final wording and functionality may be updated when applicable rules or official guidance change.
