Professional Profile Guidelines

Profiles are informational and are published only after administrative review.

Permitted: factual professional information, qualification, enrolment information, practice areas, courts/forums and professional links that can be appropriately published.
Not permitted: rankings, “best lawyer” or “No. 1” claims, success-rate claims, guarantees, misleading credentials, confidential/client information, impersonation or prohibited promotional content.
Review process: Submission → administrative review → publication or return for changes. Material changes may be re-reviewed. “Reviewed for Publication” is an editorial status and is not certification or endorsement.
Privacy: Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, residential address, client-confidential records or other unnecessary sensitive documents in the public form. Review email is used for submission communication and is not displayed publicly.
