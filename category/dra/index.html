Recovery work begins with authority, identity and responsible conduct.

This guide focuses on role boundaries, borrower interaction, confidentiality, communication, documentation and escalation within the applicable regulatory framework.

FIELD CONDUCT

What responsible recovery looks like

The following is a practical awareness summary of the conduct principles reflected in the current DRA material.

DO

Use a documented process

  • Carry appropriate authorisation and identification.
  • Communicate fairly and lawfully.
  • Protect customer confidentiality.
  • Maintain records and receipts.
  • Use the regulated entity's grievance / escalation mechanism.
AVOID

Prohibited or inappropriate conduct

  • Intimidation or harassment.
  • Unwarranted intrusion into privacy.
  • Inappropriate communications.
  • Recovery calls before 8:00 a.m. or after 7:00 p.m. for overdue-loan recovery, as stated in the cited RBI directions.

QUICK TIMELINE

How the guidance evolved

Key milestones retained from the existing DRA explainer, presented as an editorial timeline.

Outsourcing

Regulated entities

Banks and other regulated entities may use service providers for recovery activity, while the regulated entity retains responsibility under applicable RBI directions.

Code of conduct

Professional behaviour

RBI directions emphasise training, customer confidentiality and fair / lawful recovery practices.

2022

Specific recovery-agent directions

RBI reiterated that regulated entities are responsible for agents and prohibited intimidation, harassment, privacy intrusion, inappropriate communications and calls before 8:00 a.m. or after 7:00 p.m. for overdue-loan recovery.

Today

Documentation & auditability

A sound field process should use proper authorisation, identification, records and escalation channels rather than informal pressure.
