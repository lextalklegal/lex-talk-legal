A MATTER MAY MOVE THROUGH LEVELS

The Legal Journey

The applicable forum and route depend on the dispute, statute, procedural posture and jurisdiction.

STEP 01

Cause / dispute

A legal issue or dispute gives rise to a proceeding.

STEP 02

Trial or original forum

The matter begins before the forum having the relevant original jurisdiction.

STEP 03

High Court / appellate forum

Further proceedings may arise through appeal, revision or constitutional jurisdiction as applicable.

STEP 04

Supreme Court / final route

In matters within its jurisdiction, the Supreme Court may form the final appellate or constitutional route.

FROM PRE-CONSTITUTION TO TODAY

Quick Timeline

A concise historical view using the existing Lex Talk Legal reference points.

1937

Federal Court

The Federal Court functioned during the pre-Constitution period and preceded the Supreme Court.

1950

Supreme Court

The Supreme Court came into existence with the Constitution on 26 January 1950 and was inaugurated on 28 January 1950.

1958

Present building

The Supreme Court moved from the old Parliament House to its present Tilak Marg building in New Delhi in 1958.

Today

Multi-level system

India operates through the Supreme Court, High Courts and District & Subordinate Courts, alongside specialised tribunals created by statute.
