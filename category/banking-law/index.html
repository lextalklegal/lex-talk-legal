Quick Timeline

1934

RBI Act

The Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 forms a foundational part of the statutory framework governing the central bank.

1949

Banking Regulation Act

The Banking Regulation Act, 1949 consolidated and amended the law relating to banking.

1993

Debt recovery tribunals

The Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act framework created a specialised tribunal mechanism for expeditious adjudication and recovery of specified bank / financial-institution debts.

2002

SARFAESI

SARFAESI created a statutory framework for securitisation, reconstruction and enforcement of security interests.

2016

IBC

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 consolidated the insolvency framework and established IBBI.
