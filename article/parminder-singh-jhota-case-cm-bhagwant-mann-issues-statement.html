Parminder Singh Jhota Case: CM Bhagwant Mann Issues Statement

Published: 15 October 2026, 12:18:32 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann has addressed the public regarding the ongoing discussions surrounding the Parminder Singh Jhota case. The official stance was shared through a post by AAP Punjab on the social media platform X.

Chief Minister's Stance on Law and Order

According to the statement attributed to the Chief Minister in the referenced post, the Punjab government maintains that any attempts to disrupt peace and stability in the state will not be tolerated under any circumstances. The remarks come amid ongoing public interest and political dialogue concerning law enforcement and the specific case details.

Context and Public Discourse

The Parminder Singh Jhota case has drawn attention across political circles in Punjab, prompting official commentary regarding the administration's approach to local security and legal procedures. While discussions continue to unfold across various platforms, the state leadership has reiterated its commitment to preserving public order.
