Sanjay Singh Slams PM Modi Over Tricolour Row and Arrests

Published: 14 October 2026, 17:11:32 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) Member of Parliament Sanjay Singh has launched a sharp critique against Prime Minister Narendra Modi regarding a controversy involving the national Tricolour. In his public statements, Singh raised pressing questions concerning patriotism, freedom of expression, and the legal treatment of citizens reportedly jailed for displaying the national flag and chanting slogans like "भारत माता की जय" (Victory to Mother India).

Allegations of Authoritarian Governance

Drawing attention to the issue through a post on the social media platform X, Singh referenced individuals named Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Dass. The AAP leader accused the central government of authoritarian practices, questioning whether the current administration's approach reflects democratic principles.

Key Issues Raised

  • Questions surrounding patriotism and the display of the national Tricolour.
  • Concerns over freedom of expression and public sloganeering.
  • Allegations of authoritarianism leveled against the government regarding the detention of citizens.
