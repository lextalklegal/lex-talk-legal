Rahul Gandhi Joins Goy Rakhonn Yatra in Goa: Calconda to Margao

Published: 18 October 2026, 17:32:36 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi participated in the Goy Rakhonn Yatra, marking the concluding leg of the statewide journey in Goa. The march covered the route from Calconda to Margao.

Focus of the Yatra

According to the video description and event details, the yatra was organised by the Indian National Congress. The programme primarily centres around issues related to Goa's land, identity, heritage, and the rights of its people.

Coverage and Attribution

Visuals and updates from the event were sourced from public channels, including a broadcast shared by Rahul Gandhi. Political statements, campaign claims, and objectives highlighted during the march remain attributable to the respective speakers and organisers.
