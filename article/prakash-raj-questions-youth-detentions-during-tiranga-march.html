Prakash Raj Questions Youth Detentions During Tiranga March

Published: 17 October 2026, 16:56:56 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Actor Prakash Raj has publicly questioned the detention of young protesters during a Tiranga march in Delhi. In his remarks, he also raised questions concerning the #HarGharTiranga campaign, drawing public attention to the actions taken against demonstrators.

Reactions and Key Issues

The statements made by the actor have sparked a wider debate regarding the boundaries of peaceful protest, the protection of freedom of expression, and the specific circumstances surrounding police detentions during public demonstrations.

Context and Verification

According to the video material, the views expressed belong entirely to the speaker. The video is presented for news reporting, public awareness, and general discussion. The circumstances and legality of any individual detentions must be assessed based on verified facts and official records rather than commentary alone.
