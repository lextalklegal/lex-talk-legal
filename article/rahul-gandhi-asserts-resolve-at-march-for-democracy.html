Rahul Gandhi Asserts Resolve at March for Democracy

Published: 13 October 2026, 09:35:02 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Following the recent March for Democracy, prominent political figure Rahul Gandhi expressed gratitude to the participants, including INDIA bloc leaders, Congress workers, and civil society organisations, for their backing of the protest.

Defending the Constitution and Democracy

In his public statement, featured through video excerpts, Gandhi asserted that citizens would not be intimidated or bullied. He called for a collective effort aimed at defending the Indian Constitution and democratic principles.

Criticism of Leadership and RSS

During his remarks, Gandhi also directed criticism toward the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) and the Prime Minister. The video highlights these assertions as part of his post-protest address.
