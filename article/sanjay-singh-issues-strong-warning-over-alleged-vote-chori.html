Sanjay Singh Issues Strong Warning Over Alleged Vote Chori

Published: 12 October 2026, 19:06:22 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) Member of Parliament Sanjay Singh recently took to social media platform X to share a strong political message concerning alleged vote tampering. Highlighting the phrase, “Dam hai kitna daman mein tere, dekh liya hai dekhenge… vote chori-seenazori nahi chalegi!”, the parliamentarian raised his voice against what he characterized as vote theft and high-handedness.

Protest Slogan and Social Media Campaign

As detailed in the source material, the political message shared by the AAP leader accompanied slogans addressing alleged irregularities. The communications utilized specific hashtags, including the call for accountability regarding electoral processes, reflecting ongoing political tensions and public-interest discussions surrounding election transparency.
