Deepender Hooda Thanks Haryana for Rahul Gandhi's March for Democracy

Published: 13 October 2026, 18:51:06 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Congress leader Deepender Singh Hooda has publicly thanked the people of Haryana and Haryana Congress workers for their active participation in the March for Democracy led by Rahul Gandhi in Delhi.

Details of the March and Objectives

According to a post on X by Hooda, the demonstration was organized to emphasize the protection of democracy, secure a fair electoral process, and demand an impartial Election Commission. The leader acknowledged the strong turnout from the state and affirmed that the ongoing political efforts to protect democratic frameworks will persist.

Context and Attribution

These remarks reflect the official statements and expressions of gratitude shared by Deepender Hooda regarding the protest event and its stated goals.
