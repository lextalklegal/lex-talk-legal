What is the DRA Exam? Debt Recovery Agent Certification Guide

Published: 18 October 2026, 18:09:50 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

For candidates looking to build a career in the debt recovery and collection field for banking and financial institutions, understanding the Debt Recovery Agent (DRA) certification is essential. This guide outlines the fundamentals of the examination process based on available community resources.

Understanding the DRA Exam

The term DRA stands for Debt Recovery Agent. The DRA Certificate Examination is a certification test conducted by the Indian Institute of Banking & Finance (IIBF). It is designed for individuals seeking professional relevance in the debt recovery and collection sector within the banking industry.

According to IIBF guidelines, the certification process generally involves completing the prescribed training, applying for the examination, and successfully passing the test.

Preparation and Community Support

Aspirants often look for guidance, study tips, and relevant updates to clear the certification. Independent aspirant networks, such as the DRA Community India WhatsApp group, facilitate discussions, preparation strategies, and connections among peers who are navigating the certification process.

  • Discussing exam preparation methods
  • Sharing timely information and updates
  • Connecting with fellow certification aspirants

Important Considerations

Aspirant groups and communities operate independently. Candidates should directly verify official exam eligibility, mandatory training requirements, registration dates, and fees through the official IIBF website. Joining any preparation community does not guarantee passing the exam or securing a job placement.
