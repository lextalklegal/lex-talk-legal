All India Bank Strike 2026: Are Banks Open on Sunday, 27 September? Check Bank Working Status, Strike Dates and Services

Lex Talk Legal · Original Blogger post


All India Bank Strike 2026: Are you wondering whether banks are open or closed on Sunday, September 27, 2026? There is an important update for bank customers across India. The Finance Ministry has announced that Public Sector Banks (PSBs) and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) will remain operational on Sunday, September 27, ahead of a proposed three-day nationwide bank strike from September 28 to September 30, 2026.

The special Sunday working arrangement has been made to ensure that customers get an additional opportunity to complete essential banking transactions before the proposed strike.

Bank Working on Sunday, 27 September 2026

Normally, Sunday is a bank holiday in India. However, September 27, 2026, is an exception because of the special arrangement announced ahead of the proposed bank strike.

According to the Ministry of Finance, all Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks will function normally on Sunday, September 27. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has also approved the operation of bank branches, offices, ATM-linked branches and currency chests on that day.

Why Are Banks Working on Sunday?

The decision has been taken because the proposed three-day nationwide bank strike starts immediately after the weekend.

The sequence is:

  • September 26, 2026 – Saturday: Fourth Saturday bank holiday

  • September 27, 2026 – Sunday: Special working day for PSBs and RRBs

  • September 28, 2026 – Monday: Proposed nationwide bank strike

  • September 29, 2026 – Tuesday: Proposed nationwide bank strike

  • September 30, 2026 – Wednesday: Proposed nationwide bank strike

The government said the Sunday opening is intended to reduce inconvenience to customers and provide access to banking services before the proposed strike.

All India Bank Strike: September 28–30, 2026

The United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) has called for a three-day nationwide bank strike from September 28 to September 30, 2026.

The unions' principal demand is implementation of a five-day banking week. The government has said that the Performance Linked Incentive (PLI) issue was placed in abeyance, while the five-day banking week remains an outstanding issue.

The government has also appealed to bank unions to defer the proposed strike and has taken measures intended to maintain essential banking services.

Which Banks Are Working on Sunday, September 27?

The special government arrangement specifically covers:

  • Public Sector Banks (PSBs)

  • Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

The RBI approval covers bank branches, offices, ATM-linked branches and currency chests for operation on September 27.

What About Private Banks?

Customers of private-sector banks should check their individual bank's branch advisory before visiting, because the government announcement specifically refers to Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks. Reporting has also noted that private-sector banks are expected to be less affected by the proposed strike.

Will ATM, UPI and Online Banking Work During the Strike?

Digital banking services are expected to remain available, although transactions that require manual bank-branch processing may face delays during the strike.

Customers can generally continue using:

  • UPI payments

  • Mobile banking

  • Internet banking

  • ATM services

  • Debit-card transactions

However, customers should distinguish between digital services and services requiring bank employees or physical branch processing. The latter may be affected during the strike.

What Should Bank Customers Do?

Customers who need urgent branch-based services should consider completing them on September 27 or before the strike begins.

This is particularly relevant for transactions or requests that require physical branch staff, documentation, verification or manual processing.

For routine payments, customers can use UPI, mobile banking, internet banking and ATMs where available.

Important Update for Customers

There has been some reported uncertainty regarding Sunday staffing because an officers' association advised its members not to report for work, despite the government's decision to keep PSBs and RRBs operational. Therefore, while the official arrangement is for these banks to function on September 27, actual branch-level operations may vary in some locations.

Customers with urgent work should therefore verify their particular branch's status before travelling.

FAQs

Is Sunday, September 27, 2026 a bank working day?

Normally Sunday is a bank holiday, but September 27, 2026 has been made a special working day for Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks ahead of the proposed three-day strike.

Are SBI, PNB and other government banks open on September 27?

Public Sector Banks have been instructed to operate on Sunday, September 27. Customers should nevertheless check their local branch for any branch-specific operational update.

Is there an All India Bank Strike in September 2026?

Yes. The UFBU has called a proposed three-day nationwide bank strike from September 28 to September 30, 2026.

Will UPI work during the bank strike?

UPI and other digital banking services are expected to continue, although transactions requiring manual intervention by bank staff may be affected or delayed.

Will ATMs work during the bank strike?

ATM services are expected to continue, although customers should be aware that cash availability can vary by location and replenishment schedules.

Why are banks opening on Sunday?

The government arranged Sunday banking to provide customers with an additional working day before the proposed September 28–30 strike, reducing the potential disruption caused by the preceding weekend holidays.

Will all private banks open on Sunday?

The special government announcement specifically covers Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks. Private-bank customers should check their bank's own notice or branch status before visiting.

Conclusion

Bank customers have an important banking window on Sunday, September 27, 2026. Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks are scheduled to remain operational despite the normal Sunday holiday, following RBI approval and the government's special arrangement.

The move comes immediately before the proposed All India Bank Strike from September 28 to September 30, 2026. Customers with urgent branch-related work should check their branch status and, where possible, complete important transactions before the strike period.

This article reflects information available on September 27, 2026. Bank branch operations and strike arrangements can change, so customers should also check their bank's latest official advisory.
