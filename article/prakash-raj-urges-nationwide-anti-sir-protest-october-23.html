Prakash Raj Urges Citizens to Join Nationwide Anti-SIR Protest on October 23

Published: 23 October 2026, 13:05:00 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Original-source news report · Original source ↗

Actor Prakash Raj has urged people to join nationwide protests on Friday, 23 October 2026. In a post on X, he shared the hashtags #ScrapSIR, #SaveDemocracy and #HarGaliJantarMantar and called on people to participate in the countrywide protest. His short post does not specify individual venues, timings or local arrangements.

What did Prakash Raj post?

In the X post embedded below, Prakash Raj wrote: “#ScrapSIR #SaveDemocracy #HarGaliJantarMantar join the country wide protest s today.” The message is a call to participate in a protest against the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. The post itself does not provide a detailed list of events or logistical instructions.

Context: nationwide anti-SIR demonstrations

Coverage published ahead of 23 October reported that the People Against SIR alliance had called for nationwide marches under the “People’s March to Reclaim Democracy” banner. Siasat reported that the mobilisation was seeking the scrapping of SIR and action concerning the Chief Election Commissioner; News9 also reported that actor-activist Prakash Raj and other public figures were among those expected to join demonstrations.

These details describe the stated aims and expected participation reported by those outlets. They should not be read as confirmation of turnout at any particular location or as an independent finding on the merits of the political demands.

What is SIR?

SIR stands for Special Intensive Revision, an exercise relating to the revision of electoral rolls. Questions about how the process is conducted, eligibility, notices, claims and objections should be assessed against the Election Commission of India’s applicable instructions and official material. Prakash Raj’s post is a protest call; it does not by itself establish the legal or factual merits of every claim made in the wider debate.

What has been confirmed—and what remains to be checked?

  • Confirmed from the supplied public post: Prakash Raj shared the three hashtags and urged people to join a countrywide protest on 23 October.
  • Reported context: News coverage described a nationwide mobilisation called by the People Against SIR alliance and outlined the demands associated with it.
  • Not specified in the post: It does not list local meeting points, timings, permissions, routes or participation figures.
  • For the official process: Readers should consult Election Commission of India notices and the directions of relevant local authorities for accurate procedural information and event arrangements.
