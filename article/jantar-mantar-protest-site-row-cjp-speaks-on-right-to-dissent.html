Jantar Mantar Protest Site Row: CJP Speaks on Right to Dissent

Published: 13 October 2026, 18:37:50 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Recent discussions on social media have brought the status of designated demonstration areas back into focus. The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), via a post on the platform X, has raised important questions regarding public assembly and freedom of expression in the capital.

Concerns Over Freedom of Expression

According to the source material, CJP argued that any potential move to denotify Jantar Mantar as a protest site aimed at silencing dissent would highlight governmental apprehension regarding public demonstrations. The commentary positions public protest as an essential pillar of a functioning democracy, protecting the constitutional rights to freedom of expression and peaceful assembly.

Context and Verification

It is important to note that the statements reflect the position and arguments of CJP. The available material does not independently establish that an official decision to denotify Jantar Mantar has actually been implemented by authorities.
