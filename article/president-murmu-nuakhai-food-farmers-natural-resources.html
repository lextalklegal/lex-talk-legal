President Murmu Says Respecting Food, Honouring Farmers and Protecting Nature Is a Collective Duty

Published: 23 October 2026, 15:30:00 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Original-source news report · Original source ↗

President Droupadi Murmu said that food is more than a means of meeting hunger: it reflects farmers’ labour, the generosity of the earth and nature’s gifts. In an X post dated 22 October 2026, the President’s official account shared this message from her address at the ‘Seva Samman Parv’ and ‘Yuva Prabodhan’ programmes held during the Nuakhai festival in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. She called respect for food, recognition of farmers’ contributions and protection of natural resources a collective responsibility.

What did the President say?

The post emphasises three connected ideas: avoid taking food for granted, recognise the work that goes into producing it, and protect the natural resources on which agriculture and communities depend. The President’s official press release records the same message from her address in Raipur on 22 October 2026.

The message was delivered in the context of Nuakhai, a harvest festival associated with gratitude for the new crop, agricultural traditions and community ties. The President described such celebrations as opportunities to reconnect with cultural roots, value social harmony and pass important traditions to future generations.

What is Nuakhai?

Nuakhai is a harvest festival celebrated particularly in western Odisha and neighbouring areas, including parts of Chhattisgarh. It is associated with welcoming the new harvest and expressing gratitude for food, nature and the people who cultivate the land. The exact customs vary across communities, but food, family and community participation are central themes.

At the Raipur programme, the President connected the festival’s agricultural meaning with wider public responsibilities: respect food, recognise farmers’ labour and protect natural resources. Her address presented the festival not only as a cultural occasion but also as a reminder of the relationship between agriculture, nature and social life.

Why the message matters for farmers and natural resources

Food production relies on a combination of farmers’ work and natural inputs, including soil, water, seed, energy and weather conditions. Protecting these resources is relevant to long-term agricultural productivity, while reducing avoidable food waste is one practical way households and institutions can show respect for the effort involved in bringing food to the table.

The President’s remarks were a general civic message rather than a new policy announcement or an agricultural data release. The press release does not set out a specific programme, target or enforcement measure in relation to food waste or natural-resource protection. Readers should therefore understand the message as an appeal for shared responsibility, not as the announcement of a new legal requirement.

The President’s message to young people

The official account of the same event says Murmu also encouraged young people to dream big, keep learning, broaden their horizons and connect personal success with service to society and the nation. She stressed honesty, integrity and sensitivity, and said nation-building also depends on the small, sincere efforts of millions of citizens.

These remarks linked the harvest-festival theme to a broader message about public service and young people’s role in community life. The official release describes the President’s address; it does not report a new law or binding directive arising from the event.

What the official record confirms

  • Event: ‘Seva Samman Parv’ and ‘Yuva Prabodhan’ during the Nuakhai festival.
  • Location and date: Raipur, Chhattisgarh, on 22 October 2026.
  • Food and farmers: The President said respecting food and acknowledging farmers’ contributions were shared responsibilities.
  • Natural resources: She connected gratitude for nature with the responsibility to protect resources.
  • Youth: She encouraged young people to align personal goals with service to society and the country.
