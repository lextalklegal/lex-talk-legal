AAP Punjab Questions BJP Over Anti-Drug Campaign and Actions

Published: 21 October 2026, 13:37:07 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Political debate surrounding the drug issue in Punjab has intensified, with the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) questioning the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) over its recent anti-drug campaign. Baltej Pannu, AAP Punjab General Secretary and Media Incharge, raised multiple questions addressing the efficacy and ground-level actions associated with the opposition's political campaign.

Key Questions Raised by AAP Punjab

According to the video description and statements attributed to Baltej Pannu, the queries directed at the BJP include:

  • Whether BJP leaders met with the families of two specific individuals mentioned in official statements by AAP.
  • What concrete ground-level steps were executed following the conclusion of the anti-drug march.
  • How necessary substantial, solid enforcement actions are alongside political rallies to effectively combat the drug crisis.

Context and Attribution

The discussions highlight the ongoing political sparring in Punjab politics concerning measures to achieve a drug-free state. Viewers should note that all political statements, allegations, and questions detailed in this report are strictly attributed to AAP Punjab and its representative, Baltej Pannu. The source material does not independently establish these claims as verified facts.
