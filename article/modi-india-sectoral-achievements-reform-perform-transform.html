PM Modi Highlights India’s Sectoral Achievements, Credits ‘Reform, Perform, Transform’

Published: 23 October 2026, 14:00:00 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Original-source news report · Original source ↗

Prime Minister Narendra Modi said India is achieving “several firsts” across sectors, while traditional areas of strength are becoming stronger and the country is expanding into new fields. In an X post on 22 October 2026, he described this as the impact of “Reform, Perform and Transform”. The message echoed his address at the NDTV World Summit 2026 in New Delhi, according to the Prime Minister’s Office website.

What did Modi say?

In the post embedded below, Modi said that India was achieving firsts across sectors, strengthening its conventional strengths and making its mark in newer areas. He credited the progress to the government’s “Reform, Perform and Transform” approach. The post is a broad summary of his message rather than a sector-by-sector data release.

Examples cited in the NDTV World Summit address

The official PMIndia summary of the 22 October address lists several examples that Modi highlighted. These are the Prime Minister’s stated points as recorded by his office; the speech summary itself is not an independent audit of each figure or of the extent to which a particular policy caused the outcome.

  • Digital economy: Modi said India ranks first in real-time digital payments and highlighted the country’s digital economy and startup ecosystem.
  • Clean energy: He said India had crossed 300 gigawatts of non-fossil electricity capacity.
  • Science and technology: He referred to the Fast Breeder Reactor achieving criticality, the first hydrogen train run and wider use of digital technology in the national Census.
  • Research and innovation: The official summary recorded his references to almost 1.5 lakh patent filings in 2025 and India’s role in global chip design.
  • Manufacturing and pharmaceuticals: He cited the restarting of domestic Penicillin G production in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, following policy support under a production-linked incentive framework.
  • Jobs, exports and livelihoods: The official account also listed claims about goods exports, seafood exports, women-led rural enterprises under the Lakhpati Didi initiative and employment under the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana.

These examples span different measures and time periods. They should be checked against the relevant official datasets and sector-specific reports before comparing performance across years or attributing a particular result to a single policy.

What does ‘Reform, Perform and Transform’ mean?

The phrase is used by the government to describe its policy and delivery approach. In broad terms, “reform” refers to changes in rules and processes, “perform” to implementation and delivery, and “transform” to longer-term changes in economic capacity and citizens’ experience. The Prime Minister’s Office has used the same framing in other official communications, including its description of the PRAGATI governance review system.

The phrase is a policy framing, not a standalone performance indicator. Assessing whether reforms have improved outcomes requires clear baselines, measurable targets, implementation data and independent evaluation. Results can also be shaped by private investment, global demand, state-level action and wider economic conditions.

Why the sectoral claims matter

The areas named in the address—digital payments, clean energy, semiconductors, AI, research, transport and pharmaceuticals—are relevant to India’s efforts to expand domestic capabilities and participate in higher-value industries. Each has its own indicators: for example, installed capacity for energy, transaction volumes for payments, fabrication and design capability for semiconductors, and output or productivity measures for manufacturing.

A sectoral claim is easiest to assess when it specifies the measurement, time period and comparison point. The official speech summary gives examples of milestones that Modi chose to highlight, while the relevant ministries, regulators and independent datasets provide the records needed to examine those indicators in more detail.
