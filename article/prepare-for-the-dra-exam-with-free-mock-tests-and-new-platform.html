Prepare for the DRA Exam with Free Mock Tests and New Platform

Published: 20 October 2026, 19:34:56 IST
By Lex Talk Legal Editorial Desk · Educational & informational coverage · Original source ↗

Lex Talk Legal has announced the launch of DRA Learning, an educational platform designed to simplify Debt Recovery Agent (DRA) exam preparation. The platform aims to help candidates prepare for the exam through easy-to-understand formats, addressing challenges faced by learners dealing with complex terminology.

Features of the DRA Learning Platform

According to the details provided, candidates visiting the platform can access several resources:

  • Free Starter Mock Tests: Aspirants can attempt practice questions and review their answers.
  • Upcoming Courses: Information regarding upcoming step-by-step course series and learning plans.
  • Learning Formats: Details on available live and recorded learning options.
  • Community Support: Connection points for legal awareness, preparation guidance, and community engagement.

Learner Discounts and Support

The platform also outlines a discount mechanism for learners. Candidates who subscribe to the Lex Talk Legal YouTube channel can share a screenshot of their subscription via WhatsApp through the website's discount section. Upon manual verification, eligible learners receive a discount code and validity details.

Important Disclaimer

The source notes that DRA Learning is an independent educational and exam-preparation resource operated by LEXBOTICS AI MEDIA LLP. It is not affiliated with the Indian Institute of Banking & Finance (IIBF). The platform does not guarantee IIBF affiliation, certification, exam success, or job placement. Candidates are advised to verify official examination rules, fees, and mandatory training requirements through official IIBF channels.
