How to Check Case Status Online in India: CNR, Case Number, Party Name and Advocate Search

Looking for case status online, CNR status, case number search or case status by party name? India's eCourts services provide several official search routes. This guide explains how to use them.

Editorial note: This is an educational overview, not legal advice. Statutory provisions, rules, notifications, judicial decisions and facts of individual matters can change or interact differently depending on the case. Readers should verify the current official text and obtain case-specific advice where required.

What is CNR?

CNR means Case Number Record. The official eCourts Services information states that the CNR is a unique identification number assigned to a case for tracking and that users can search case details using the CNR. The eCourts help material instructs users to enter the 16-character alphanumeric CNR without spaces or hyphens when using the CNR search.

How to check case status using CNR

  1. Open the official eCourts Services portal.
  2. Select the CNR search option.
  3. Enter the 16-character alphanumeric CNR without spaces or hyphens.
  4. Complete the captcha where required.
  5. Submit the search.
  6. Review the case details and available history.

Depending on the court and the available record, case history can include case details, status, hearing dates, orders and other information.

What if I do not know the CNR number?

eCourts provides alternative search options. Official eCourts help guidance lists searches by case number, filing number, party name, advocate name, FIR number, Act and case type, subject to the service and court involved.

How to search by party name

The eCourts help guide allows party-name searches using a full or partial name. The official guide states that a partial party-name search can use at least three characters in the relevant service. The party may appear as petitioner, plaintiff, complainant, respondent, defendant, appellant, accused or another extra party depending on the case record.

Choose the relevant court complex and registration year where the service asks for them, select the case status where required, and review the matching results before opening a specific case.

How to search by advocate name

The eCourts system provides an advocate search in its case-status services. Official guidance lists search by advocate name, Bar Registration Number, or date-wise case list in the applicable service.

Where multiple advocates have similar names, use the available filters and verify the resulting case record carefully.

How to search by case number

For a case-number search, the official High Court and district services generally ask users to select the relevant court, case type and registration year and then enter the case number. The exact screen varies between services.

How to search by FIR number

eCourts also provides FIR-number search in the relevant services. The official help guide explains that users may be required to select a police station, enter FIR number and year, choose pending/disposed/both and complete the captcha.

Can I search by Act?

Yes. eCourts describes an Act-based case search. The service can list cases under an Act and may allow selection of a specific Act type and section where the facility is provided.

What information can case history show?

The official eCourts Services App FAQ states that case history can include case status, hearing dates, judge details, party and advocate information, FIR details and court proceedings. The information displayed varies with the court, case and data available on the portal.

Is the online status always the same as the latest courtroom event?

Online court systems are information services and can depend on the underlying data being entered and updated. For a hearing that has just taken place, the online status may not immediately reflect every development. Where the matter is urgent, verify the relevant order/cause list or obtain information from the appropriate court source.
