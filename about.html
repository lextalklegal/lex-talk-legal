OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

Two commitments shape the platform: make legal information easier to understand, and build a responsible place to find it.

01MISSION

Make legal information easier to understand.

Our mission is to simplify legal information and make meaningful legal developments accessible, understandable and easier to follow for everyone.

Clear information should not require a reader to already speak the language of law.
02VISION

Build a useful, accessible legal-information platform.

Our vision is to build a credible, accessible and technology-enabled legal information platform that helps people understand law, follow the justice system and navigate important legal developments with greater clarity.

Use technology for access and speed — keep responsibility with the editorial process.
EDITORIAL FOUNDATION

Six principles. One standard.

These principles guide the presentation of reporting, explainers, public resources and correction pathways.

01

Clarity

Complex developments should be presented in a way that preserves their essential meaning.

02

Context

Important legal developments benefit from relevant background, chronology and practical context.

03

Responsibility

Legal information can affect real decisions, so presentation should be careful and responsible.

04

Attribution

Where information comes from identifiable orders, notices or official sources, the source should be recognised appropriately.

05

Correction

Factual mistakes should have a transparent route for review, correction and acknowledgement.

06

Accessibility

Legal information should be easier to access for people who are not already familiar with legal terminology.

HOW WE WORK

From source to reader.

A simple editorial rhythm helps keep legal information structured, contextual and easier to verify.

01

Identify

Find the relevant development, order, notice, announcement or source material.

02

Contextualise

Place the development in the surrounding legal, procedural or public context.

03

Present Clearly

Organise information into readable headlines, summaries, explainers and practical sections.

04

Review & Correct

Revisit factual issues when identified and use an appropriate correction or grievance pathway.

TECHNOLOGY & AI

Technology-assisted.
Human-responsible.

Technology can improve speed, structure and accessibility. It does not remove the need for editorial responsibility.

OUR OBJECTIVEUse technology to improve speed, structure and accessibility while maintaining responsibility for the information we publish.
WORKING PRINCIPLE

Speed should support clarity — not replace it.

  • Technology may assist research and organisation.
  • Source verification remains important.
  • Human editorial judgement remains part of the publishing process.
  • Corrections should remain possible when factual issues are identified.
