01 / THE CHANGE

AQI 179 → 244

A reported move from Moderate to Poor

On 21 October, DD News reported Delhi’s AQI at 244 at 2 PM, compared with 179 on the previous day. The time of the reading matters: this is a reported snapshot, not proof that every NCR location had the same AQI.

VIDEO SCRIPT · HINGLISH

Voiceover cues for your recording

Target runtime: about 3–4 minutes. Each card maps to the matching animation scene. Read naturally; the script deliberately separates reported facts from legal interpretation.

00:00–00:25 · Scene 1

Hook: AQI ka jump

“Delhi ki hawa ka AQI 20 October ko 179 tha. DD News ke mutabik 21 October ko dopahar 2 baje ye 244 report hua. Iska matlab category Moderate se Poor hui. Lekin ye ek time-stamped reading hai—har NCR location ka same number nahi.”

00:25–00:55 · Scene 2

AQI aur GRAP ka simple difference

“AQI percentage nahi hota. Ye pollutants ke data ko public-friendly index mein dikhata hai. 201 se 300 Poor category hai, aur GRAP schedule isi band ko Stage-I se jodta hai. Current stage aur exact restrictions ke liye official order check karna zaroori hai.”

00:55–01:30 · Scene 3

Stage-I ka kaam

“Stage-I ka practical focus teen areas par hai: road dust control, garbage aur construction waste ki timely removal, aur construction sites par dust ko bahar failne se rokna. Sirf announcement se nahi—implementation se farq padega.”

01:30–02:00 · Scene 4

Kaun responsible hai?

“CAQM framework set karta hai, par har kaam ek department nahi karta. Roads, waste collection, site management aur enforcement ki responsibilities relevant schedule mein alag-alag agencies ko di jaati hain.”

02:00–02:35 · Scene 5

Public kya kare?

“Apne area ka latest AQI check karein, waste openly burn na karein, vehicle ko unnecessary idle na rakhein. Dust ya illegal dumping dikhe toh safe distance se location, time aur evidence ke saath official complaint channel use karein.”

02:35–03:05 · Scene 6

Rumours se bachein

“GRAP Stage-I ka matlab automatically har school band ya har private car banned nahi. Kisi bhi ban ko share karne se pehle order ka exact scope, effective date aur exemptions padhein.”

03:05–03:35 · Scene 7

Legal close

“Pollution alert ko samajhne ka sahi tareeka hai: AQI dekho, official order padho, apne par lagu rule samjho aur update check karte raho. Source links article mein hain. Law, context aur clarity ke liye Lex Talk Legal ko follow karein.”

Sources: CAQM (latest GRAP schedule/directions) · CPCB National AQI framework · DD News original post · The Indian Express · The Economic Times. Third-party embeds or headlines are not a substitute for reading the underlying official order.
