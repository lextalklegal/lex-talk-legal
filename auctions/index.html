Before You Bid

  • Read the original sale/auction notice.
  • Verify title, possession, dues, encumbrances and statutory disclosures independently.
  • Check EMD, eligibility, KYC and bid-deadline requirements.
  • Understand inspection, sale terms and payment schedule.
