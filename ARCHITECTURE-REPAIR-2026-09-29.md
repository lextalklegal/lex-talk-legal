# Lex Talk Legal — Stable Maintenance Architecture

## Why pages were reverting

The repository had two different `build_site.py` files. The scheduled workflow used `scripts/build_site.py`, while newer page-design logic existed in another copy. In addition, the old scheduled sync used Playwright/OneCourt, rebuilt unrelated pages, rewrote configuration, and staged the entire repository. Page-specific CSS was also generated inconsistently, so approved designs could be overwritten by later syncs.

## Current source-of-truth model

- `scripts/build_site.py` is the only editorial builder.
- `templates/` holds generated-page layouts.
- `assets/pages/` holds page-specific CSS.
- Static pages such as About and Contact are not regenerated by editorial sync.
- `data/vc_links.json` is the manual source of truth for courtroom/VC links.
- `data/auctions.json` is the manual source of truth for auctions.
- `data/jobs.json` is the manual source of truth for jobs.
- `deploy-worker.yml` is the only workflow that calls Wrangler deploy.

## Workflow behaviour

### Editorial — every 30 minutes
Blogger + YouTube only. It updates article data, article pages, editorial categories, Videos, homepage selection and sitemap. It does not fetch VC links, auctions or jobs.

### VC — on demand
Update `data/vc_links.json` and commit it. `update-vc.yml` rebuilds only `/courtrooms/`. Meeting IDs, passwords, verification dates and notes are optional per entry.

### Auctions — on demand
Update `data/auctions.json` and commit it. `update-auctions.yml` rebuilds only `/auctions/`.

### Jobs — on demand
Update `data/jobs.json` and commit it. `update-jobs.yml` rebuilds only `/jobs/`.

### Deployment
Any successful push to `main` triggers `deploy-worker.yml`. This is the only workflow that deploys the Worker.

## Design safety

The global header, navigation/menu and footer are shared and intentionally left untouched by page-specific design work. A single page can be redesigned by changing its own template/CSS without replacing the complete build script.

## Editorial archive safety

The editorial builder merges recent Blogger/YouTube results into the local data archive instead of deleting older entries that are outside a limited feed window. This avoids losing older article pages during scheduled syncs.

## Low-noise scheduling

Homepage timestamps are derived from source publication dates instead of build time. Sitemap entries use deterministic last-modified dates where available. This prevents no-content-change scheduled runs from creating pointless commits and redeployments.
